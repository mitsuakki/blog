# about

**Alexis M. Daugé**. Software and reverse engineer. Cognitive science student
at [ENSC Bordeaux INP](https://ensc.bordeaux-inp.fr/).

## what i do

- **Binary Ninja plugin dev:** [deobfninja](https://github.com/mitsuakki/deobfninja),
  a C++ deobfuscation plugin. Targets opaque predicates, control flow flattening,
  mixed boolean arithmetic.
- **MCP + RE tooling:** [reverse-mcp](https://github.com/mitsuakki/reverse-mcp),
  Docker-based reverse engineering lab. Ghidra, radare2, angr, and a shell,
  all as MCP servers for AI agents.
- **OS internals:** [x86-kernel](https://github.com/mitsuakki/x86-kernel),
  from-scratch x86_64 kernel. Bare metal, NASM, QEMU. No AI used.
- **PDB reversing:** [bds-sdk-generator](https://github.com/sarumc/bds-sdk-generator)
  (archived). Reconstructing PDB symbols into compilable C headers.
